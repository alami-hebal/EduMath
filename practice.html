Part 1: Row Operations & Gaussian Elimination

Questions 1-5

1. Apply the elementary row operation $R_1 \leftrightarrow R_2$ to the matrix:

$$ A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 \end{bmatrix} $$

2. Apply the scalar row operation $2R_1$ to matrix $B$:

$$ B = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2\\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} $$

3. Apply the row replacement operation $R_2 - 2R_1$ to matrix $C$:

$$ C = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 5\\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} $$

4. Solve the following system using Gaussian or Gauss-Jordan elimination:

$2x+y-z=8$
$-3x-y+2z=-11$
$-2x+y+2z=3$

5. Solve the following system of equations:

$3x-y=9$
$2x+y=4$

Part 2: Elementary & Equivalent Matrices

Questions 6-10

6. Determine the inverse of the elementary matrix $E$ given by the row operation $R_2 \leftrightarrow R_3$:

$$ E = \begin{bmatrix} 1&0&0\\ 0&0&1\\ 0&1&0 \end{bmatrix} $$

7. Solve the following system by row reduction:

$x+2y=8$
$3x+4y=17$

8. Use row reduction to determine whether the following system has a solution:

$x+y=2$
$2x+2y=5$

9. Solve the following system in parametric form:

$x+2y+z=4$
$2x+4y+2z=8$

10. Determine whether the following two augmented matrices represent equivalent systems:

$$ M_1= \begin{bmatrix} 1&2&|&5\\ 0&1&|&3 \end{bmatrix} $$
$$ M_2= \begin{bmatrix} 1&0&|&-1\\ 0&1&|& 3 \end{bmatrix} $$
